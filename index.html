Our Purpose

Institutional discipline
Private wealth access

Individual investors cannot buy what institutions buy. Pension funds, endowments, and sovereign wealth funds reach a caliber of asset, economics, and service that rarely reaches private investors, however substantial. The gap shows up at the worst possible moment. When you sell an investment property, a business, or another appreciated asset, you face one of the largest tax events of your life with a thin set of options in front of you.
Sightbridge Capital Partners was founded in 2025 in Santa Monica, CA, to close that gap. We design tax-advantaged real estate programs with institutional real estate owner-operators and distribute them through the wealth management community. Where taxation and real estate intersect, the right structure matters as much as the right asset. We connect investors to that destination.

A platform of tax-advantaged real estate strategies

Sightbridge designs, distributes, and services tax-advantaged real estate programs across three strategies. Each is built with the underwriting, terms, and economics an institutional investor would require.

1031 Exchanges

Defer capital gains on the sale of investment real estate by reinvesting through institutional-quality Delaware Statutory Trust programs.

Explore 1031 Exchanges →

721 UPREITs

A 1031 Exchange into a DST, with the potential for a subsequent 721 contribution into a REIT operating partnership. If that contribution occurs, appreciated real estate becomes an interest in a diversified, professionally managed portfolio.

Explore 721 UPREITs →

Opportunity Zones

Defer recognized capital gains by investing in designated Opportunity Zones through Qualified Opportunity Fund programs.

Explore Opportunity Zones →

Leadership

Built by operators

Sightbridge is led by principals with complementary experience across institutional real estate, capital markets, and private wealth distribution. Collectively, they bring 95 years of combined experience, have been involved in approximately $45 billion of real estate transactions, and have structured, distributed, and serviced 131 investment products. These figures reflect the prior experience of Sightbridge principals.

The team's background shapes how Sightbridge approaches every program: disciplined underwriting, aligned economics, and a service model built around the investor and their advisor.
